Privacy Policy — Eden Save Sync

Last updated: November 2026 · Back to Home

1. Overview

Eden Save Sync (package name com.jotasoft.edensavesync) is a free, open-source Android application developed to help users back up and synchronize their own emulator savegame files between their Android devices using their personal Google Drive account.

Eden Save Sync operates directly between your Android device and your personal Google Drive account. The developer does not operate any backend servers and does not have access to your Google account, tokens, or files.

2. Google User Data Accessed

When you choose to sign in with Google, Eden Save Sync requests only the following Google Drive API scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — Per-file access limited strictly to files and folders created or opened by Eden Save Sync.

Specifically, the application creates a single folder named EdenSaveSync in your Google Drive and stores compressed .zip backups of your game save files along with minimal synchronization metadata (title ID, game name, SHA-256 content hash, save timestamp, and user-chosen device label).

Eden Save Sync cannot access, read, list, modify, or delete any other files, photos, documents, or folders in your Google Drive.

3. How We Use Google User Data

Google user data is used exclusively to provide the core synchronization functionality requested by the user:

  • Checking whether your local save files or your Google Drive backup files are newer.
  • Uploading your save files to your own Google Drive EdenSaveSync folder.
  • Downloading your save files from your own Google Drive to your other Android devices.
  • Refreshing your OAuth 2.0 access token automatically on your device so scheduled background synchronization can run without requiring repeated manual logins.

4. Data Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

  • No third-party sharing: Eden Save Sync does not share, transfer, sell, or disclose Google user data to the developer or any third party.
  • No advertising or analytics: The app contains no ads, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no telemetry.
  • Google API Limited Use Compliance: Eden Save Sync's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

  • OAuth 2.0 tokens (access_token and refresh_token) and app settings are stored locally on your Android device in private application storage (SharedPreferences with MODE_PRIVATE).
  • All network requests to Google OAuth and Google Drive APIs use encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Your savegame backups remain in your personal Google Drive for as long as you choose to keep them. You have full ownership and control over those files at all times.

6. Data Deletion and Revoking Access

You can remove your data and revoke access at any time:

  • Inside the app: tap Google Drive → Disconnect Google Drive account to immediately delete all stored OAuth tokens from your device.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local app data and local safety backups.
  • Delete the EdenSaveSync folder directly in Google Drive to erase all cloud backups.
  • Revoke the app's permission from your Google Account Third-Party Connections page.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how Eden Save Sync handles your data, please open an issue on the public GitHub repository: https://github.com/jhuerta27/EdenSaveSync/issues.

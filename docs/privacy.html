Privacy Policy — Eden Save Sync

Effective date & Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Back to Home

1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Eden Save Sync (Android package name com.jotasoft.edensavesync, developed by JotaSoft) accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the application.

Eden Save Sync is a free, open-source Android utility designed to help users back up and synchronize their own emulator savegame files across their personal Android devices using their own Google Drive account.

Zero Developer Servers: Eden Save Sync communicates directly between your Android device and Google's official OAuth 2.0 and Google Drive APIs. The developer does not operate any backend servers, cloud databases, or proxy services, and never receives, stores, or views your Google account credentials, OAuth tokens, or savegame files.

2. Google User Data Accessed

Signing in with Google is entirely optional and initiated only when you choose to connect your Google Drive account. When you authorize Eden Save Sync, the application requests strictly a single, minimal, per-file Google Drive API scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — Grants per-file access limited exclusively to files and folders created or opened by Eden Save Sync itself.

What Eden Save Sync Creates and Accesses in Your Google Drive

  • A dedicated folder named EdenSaveSync inside your personal Google Drive.
  • Compressed .zip backup archives of your emulator savegame files that you choose to synchronize.
  • Minimal synchronization metadata stored alongside your backups (game title ID, game display name, SHA-256 content hash, save modification timestamp, and a user-defined device label) to accurately detect which device has the newest save data.

What Eden Save Sync Does NOT Access or Collect

  • Eden Save Sync does not request email, profile, openid, or broad Google Drive scopes (such as drive or drive.readonly).
  • Eden Save Sync cannot view, list, read, modify, or delete any other files, documents, spreadsheets, photos, or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Eden Save Sync does not collect personal identifiers, contact lists, precise or approximate location data, advertising IDs, crash reports, or usage analytics.

3. Local Device Permissions and On-Device Data

To synchronize local save files on your Android device, Eden Save Sync uses standard Android system capabilities strictly on-device:

  • User-Selected Storage Access (Storage Access Framework / optional Shizuku): Used exclusively to read and write savegame files in the emulator directory explicitly selected by the user (such as Eden or Peanuts Native) and to keep up to 5 local .zip safety backups before overwriting any local save.
  • Network & Background Sync Permissions: Used solely to verify internet connectivity, run optional user-configured daily scheduled sync or automatic background sync, and display optional system notifications showing sync results or conflicts.

None of your local device files or settings are ever transmitted to the developer or any third party; synchronized save files are transferred exclusively to your own Google Drive account.

4. How We Use Google User Data

Google user data accessed through the Google Drive API is used solely to provide the core user-facing synchronization features requested by you:

  • Comparing SHA-256 hashes and modification timestamps between your local device saves and your Google Drive backups to determine whether an upload, download, or conflict resolution is needed.
  • Uploading compressed savegame backups from your device to your personal EdenSaveSync folder in Google Drive.
  • Downloading compressed savegame backups from your personal EdenSaveSync folder to your other Android devices.
  • Refreshing your OAuth 2.0 access token automatically on your device using your locally stored refresh token so optional background synchronization can operate without requiring repeated manual sign-ins.

Eden Save Sync never uses Google user data for targeted advertising, marketing, user profiling, data brokering, creditworthiness assessment, or any purpose unrelated to synchronizing your savegame files.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Compliance

Eden Save Sync's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • No AI or Machine Learning Training: Eden Save Sync explicitly affirms that Google Workspace and Google Drive API data is never used to develop, improve, or train generalized Artificial Intelligence (AI) or Machine Learning (ML) models.
  • No Human Access: Because all synchronization occurs directly between your device and your personal Google Drive without developer servers, neither the developer nor any third party has human access to your Google user data or save files.

6. Data Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

  • No Third-Party Sharing: Eden Save Sync does not share, transfer, sell, rent, or disclose Google user data or local app data to the developer or any third party.
  • No Advertising or Analytics SDKs: The application contains zero ads, zero third-party analytics libraries, zero tracking SDKs, and zero telemetry.
  • Open-Source Transparency: The complete source code of Eden Save Sync is publicly auditable at https://github.com/jhuerta27/EdenSaveSync.

7. Data Storage and Security Mechanisms

We apply strict technical safeguards to protect your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between Eden Save Sync, Google OAuth 2.0 endpoints, and the Google Drive API are encrypted using HTTPS (TLS).
  • Isolated On-Device Token Storage: OAuth 2.0 tokens (access_token and refresh_token) and application settings are stored exclusively on your Android device inside Android's private application sandbox (SharedPreferences configured with Context.MODE_PRIVATE), inaccessible to other installed apps on non-rooted devices.
  • User-Owned Cloud Storage: Your savegame backups are stored exclusively in your personal Google Drive account and remain protected by Google's security infrastructure and your own Google account authentication (including 2-Step Verification if enabled).

8. Data Retention, Deletion, and Revoking Access

Data Retention

  • Developer Retention: None. The developer does not collect or retain any user data.
  • On-Device Data: OAuth 2.0 tokens, preferences, and local safety backups remain on your device only for as long as you keep your Google account connected and the app installed.
  • Google Drive Data: Savegame backups in your EdenSaveSync folder remain in your personal Google Drive for as long as you choose to keep them. You retain full ownership and control over those files at all times.

How to Delete Your Data and Revoke Access

You can delete all associated data and revoke Google Drive access at any time:

  • Disconnect inside the app: Open Eden Save Sync and tap Google Drive → Disconnect Google Drive account to immediately erase all stored OAuth tokens from your device.
  • Uninstall the app: Uninstalling Eden Save Sync permanently removes all local app data, preferences, tokens, and local safety backups from your device.
  • Delete cloud backups: Delete the EdenSaveSync folder directly in Google Drive to permanently erase all cloud save backups.
  • Revoke Google Account permission: Remove Eden Save Sync's access at any time from your Google Account Third-Party Connections page.

9. Children's Privacy

Eden Save Sync does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and does not collect personal information from users of any age. If you believe any information has been inadvertently shared with us via support correspondence, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.
